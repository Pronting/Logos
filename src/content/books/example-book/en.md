Highly recommended introductory economics reading. The author builds a comprehensive framework for understanding how China's economy operates, making it valuable for both economics undergraduates and general readers.

A rigorous conceptual framework helps clarify problems, but it often leads people to mistake the problem for the answer. Social sciences always seek universally applicable laws, but this mindset needs to mature. Don't underestimate the complexity of economic reality, nor overestimate the quality of scientific tools.
